Privacy policy

Research Project Tracker · Dr Muhammad Emmamally, MBChB, PhD · Last updated 24 October 2026

What the tracker stores

  • Your account: your email address, your name, and your password (stored only in scrambled form by our sign-in provider).
  • What you enter: projects, tasks, team names and roles, next actions, journal details, notes, meetings and settings.
  • Sharing: who you share projects with and the invitations you send (the invitee's email address).

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Who can see it

  • Your projects are private to you unless you share one. People you share a project with can see and edit that project only.
  • Your meetings and settings are visible only to you.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Supabase, which provides the database and sign-in, and is sent over encrypted connections. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Sign-in and invitation emails are sent through the email service set up for the tracker.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time: sign in, then choose Delete account at the bottom of the menu. This permanently removes your account, your projects (for everyone they were shared with), your meetings and settings. Projects other people shared with you stay with them. You can export a backup first with Export backup.

Children

The tracker is meant for researchers and isn't directed at children under 13.
