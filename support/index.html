Research Project Tracker · Dr Muhammad Emmamally, MBChB, PhD

Getting started

  • Create an account with your email address, then add a project with + New project.
  • Choose the study type: reviews and primary studies have their own stages.
  • Add your team, a next action, and tasks with start and due dates.
  • The Dashboard shows what needs your attention this week, a calendar, and your meetings.

Sharing a project

Open the project, scroll to Sharing, and enter your co-author's email address. They get an invitation by email and on their dashboard, and can accept or decline. If they don't have an account yet, the invitation waits for them after they sign up with that email address.

Meetings

Add one-off or repeating meetings on the Meetings tab. Add to my calendar puts a meeting, repeats included, into your phone's calendar.

Forgotten password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password? and follow the link in the email.

Deleting your account

Choose Delete account at the bottom of the menu. See the privacy policy for what's removed.
