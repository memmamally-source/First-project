## Promotional text (170 characters max, optional)

See what needs your attention this week across every paper, share projects with co-authors, and keep research meetings in one place.

## Description

Research Tracker keeps every paper in one place, from the first idea to submission and revisions.

WHAT NEEDS MY ATTENTION THIS WEEK?
The dashboard pulls together what's overdue and due soon across all your projects, with a calendar, a 30-day deadline timeline and your upcoming meetings.

BUILT FOR RESEARCH
• Reviews and primary studies tracked with their own stages: protocol, screening, extraction or data collection, analysis, writing, submitted
• Study types from meta-analysis and scoping review to cohort, cross-sectional, RCT and qualitative
• Target journal, submission date and revision deadline

TEAMS AND TASKS
• First authors, co-authors and acknowledged contributors
• Tasks with an owner, a start date and a due date, shown as a timeline
• A next action for each paper that shows who you're waiting for

SHARE WITH CO-AUTHORS
Invite a co-author by email to a single project. They accept from their own dashboard, and your other projects stay private.

MEETINGS
Add one-off or repeating research meetings (weekly, every two weeks, monthly) and add them to your phone's calendar.

Your data syncs securely between your phone and the website, and you can export a backup or delete your account at any time.
